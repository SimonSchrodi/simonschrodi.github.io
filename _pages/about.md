Hi and welcome to my (Simon Schrodi's) personal website!

### About me

I am a 5th year PhD student at the University of Freiburg, working in the area of explainable machine learning advised by [Prof. Thomas Brox](https://lmb.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/people/brox/). My research lies at the intersection of the science of deep learning, interpretability, and AI safety. In particular, I investigate how AI models learn and generalize, and how this shapes their behavior using controlled experiments and mechanistic analysis. My broader interests also include machine learning for climate science and automated machine learning. You can find some of my works under the Publications page or on [my Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=yC-y0PEAAAAJ&hl=en).

I am also a research scholar at MATS 9, mentored by Alex Cloud, Cem Anil, and Arthur Conmy. My work there examines the limitations of pretraining safety methods and how pretraining shapes models' propensity for agentic misalignment.

### My academic background
I started my academic journey with a BEng in Computer Engineering (1.2, equal to A) at DHBW Karlsruhe as part of a dual study program at [SICK AG](https://www.sick.com/de/en/). At SICK AG, I worked on various deep learning solutions for their sensor systems used in the logistics industry. Afterwards, I did my MSc (_summa cum laude_, 1.0, equal to A) in Computer Science at the University of Freiburg. During my master's studies, I did research on the robustness of optical flow networks (see [here](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Schrodi_Towards_Understanding_Adversarial_Robustness_of_Optical_Flow_Networks_CVPR_2022_paper.html)) and automated machine learning (see [here](https://openreview.net/forum?id=Hpt1i5j6wh) and [here](https://openreview.net/forum?id=yEGlj93aLFY)). I also participated in the CVPR 2021 - NAS Competition Track 3, where we won 2nd place (see [here](https://competitions.codalab.org/competitions/29853#results)).
